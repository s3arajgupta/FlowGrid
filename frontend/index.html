FIRST PRINCIPLES: WHY RAW VEHICLE COUNTS FAIL IN ASEAN

In Vietnam, 80% of traffic consists of motorcycles. Comparing two approaches with raw counts creates severe distortions:

Approach A: Motorcycle Dense (Nguyen Hue)

🏍️ x40 Motorcycles 🚗 x5 Cars
  • Raw Count: 45 vehicles (looks huge!)
  • FlowGrid PCU: (40 × 0.5) + (5 × 1.0) = 25.0 PCU
  • Road Footprint: Small, compact, clears fast

Approach B: Heavy Freight (Le Loi)

🚚 x2 Trucks 🚌 x2 Buses 🚗 x8 Cars
  • Raw Count: 12 vehicles (looks small!)
  • FlowGrid PCU: (2 × 3.0) + (2 × 2.5) + (8 × 1.0) = 19.0 PCU
  • Road Footprint: Massive physical space, slow acceleration
🎯 The Verdict: Raw counts suggest Approach A needs 3.75x more green time than Approach B. FlowGrid's PCU engine reveals the true capacity demand ratio is only 1.32 : 1.00. FlowGrid prevents starvation of critical public transit and freight routes.
